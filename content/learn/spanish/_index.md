## 🌎 About Spanish for English Speakers

- **500M+ native speakers** — 2nd most spoken language globally by native speakers
- **Romance language** — Latin descendant, cognate-rich with English (animal, capital, hospital, etc.)
- **20+ countries** where Spanish is official — Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, and most of Latin America
- **Phonetically friendly** — Spanish spelling is largely phonetic; pronunciation rules are consistent
- **Two main dialects** — Castilian (Spain, with /θ/ for "z" and "c") and Latin American (with /s/) — both mutually intelligible

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Week 1:** Learn the alphabet (it's phonetic) + basic greetings (Hola, ¿Cómo estás?)
2. **Week 2:** Numbers 1–100 + days of the week + basic food vocabulary
3. **Week 3:** Present tense + common verbs (ser, estar, tener, ir, hacer)
4. **Month 2:** Reflexive verbs + prepositions + start building sentence patterns
5. **Month 3+:** Past tenses (preterite + imperfect) + reading short articles
