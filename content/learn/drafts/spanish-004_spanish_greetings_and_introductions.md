# Spanish Greetings and Introductions

## 🇰🇷 한국어 요약

### 핵심
스페인어 인사말과 자기소개는 대화의 시작입니다. 영어와 달리 시간대별 인사말이 다르고, 정중함의 정도에 따라 표현이 달라집니다.

### 구조
- **시간대별 인사**: Buenos días (아침) → Buenas tardes (오후) → Buenas noches (밤/작별)
- **이름 묻기/말하기**: ¿Cómo te llamas? → Me llamo [이름] / Soy [이름]
- **안부 묻기**: ¿Cómo estás? (친근) vs ¿Cómo está usted? (정중)

### 주의사항
1. **Buenas noches**는 인사이자 작별 인사 둘 다 가능
2. **Tú** (친근) vs **Usted** (정중) 구분이 필수
3. 스페인에서는 **vosotros** 사용, 중남미에서는 **ustedes** 사용

### 다음 단계
기본 인사를 익힌 후 → 날씨 대화, 취미 묻기, 전화 통화 표현으로 확장

## Introduction

**What is this?**

Spanish greetings and introductions are the essential building blocks for starting any conversation in Spanish. Just like in English, you need to say "hello," ask "how are you," and introduce yourself. However, Spanish has some important differences: greetings change based on the time of day, and you must choose between formal and informal language depending on who you're talking to.

**Why it matters for learners**

These are the very first words you'll speak in Spanish. Mastering them gives you confidence to start real conversations immediately. They're also the foundation for all future social interactions—making friends, ordering food, asking for directions, and more.

**Quick comparison to English**

| English | Spanish Difference |
|---------|-------------------|
| "Hello" works anytime | Spanish has **3 time-specific greetings** |
| "You" is the same for everyone | Spanish has **tú (informal) vs. usted (formal)** |
| "Good night" = bedtime only | **Buenas noches** = greeting AND goodbye at night |

## Core Explanation

### The Three Time-Based Greetings

Spanish divides the day into three parts, and each has its own greeting:

**1. Buenos días** — *Good morning*  
Used from sunrise until around noon (12:00 PM).  
Pronunciation: *BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs*

**2. Buenas tardes** — *Good afternoon*  
Used from noon until sunset (roughly 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM).  
Pronunciation: *BWEH-nahs TAR-dehs*

**3. Buenas noches** — *Good evening / Good night*  
Used after sunset. **Important:** This works as both a greeting ("Good evening") AND a farewell ("Good night").  
Pronunciation: *BWEH-nahs NOH-chehs*

> **Cultural Note:** In Spain, "tardes" can extend later (until 9-10 PM) because dinner is late. In Latin America, the switch to "noches" often happens earlier (around 7-8 PM).

### Asking and Giving Names

**To ask someone's name:**

| Spanish | English | When to Use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **¿Cómo te llamas?** | What's your name? (informal) | Friends, peers, children |
| **¿Cómo se llama?** | What's your name? (formal) | Elders, strangers, professionals |

**To say your name — three common ways:**

1. **Me llamo [Name]** — *My name is [Name]* (Most common, neutral)
2. **Soy [Name]** — *I'm [Name]* (Casual, very common)
3. **Mi nombre es [Name]** — *My name is [Name]* (Slightly more formal)

> **Grammar Note:** *Me llamo* literally means "I call myself." It's a reflexive verb (*llamarse*). Don't worry about the grammar yet—just memorize the phrase!

---

### Asking "How Are You?"

Spanish distinguishes between informal and formal "you":

| Spanish | English | Use With |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **¿Cómo estás?** | How are you? | Friends, family, peers, children (tú) |
| **¿Cómo está usted?** | How are you? | Strangers, elders, bosses, formal situations (usted) |
| **¿Qué tal?** | How's it going? | Very casual, both tú and usted contexts |
| **¿Cómo te va?** | How's it going for you? | Casual, friends (tú) |

**Common responses:**

- **Bien, gracias.** — *Good, thanks.*
- **Muy bien.** — *Very good.*
- **Regular.** — *So-so / Okay.*
- **Mal.** — *Bad.*
- **¿Y tú? / ¿Y usted?** — *And you?* (informal / formal)

---

### Saying Goodbye

| Spanish | English | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Adiós** | Goodbye | Universal, any situation |
| **Hasta luego** | See you later | Casual, implies you'll meet again |
| **Hasta mañana** | See you tomorrow | Casual, specific timeframe |
| **Nos vemos** | See you / We'll see each other | Very casual, friends |
| **Chao / Chau** | Bye | Very informal, borrowed from Italian |
| **Buenas noches** | Good night | Evening farewell (also greeting!) |

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Using "Buenas noches" only for bedtime
❌ **Mistake:** Saying *Buenas noches* only when going to sleep.  
✅ **Correct:** Use it as a greeting after sunset too.  
*Why:* In Spanish, *Buenas noches* = "Good evening" (greeting) AND "Good night" (farewell).
