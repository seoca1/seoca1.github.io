# Spanish Present Tense Regular Verbs

## 🇰🇷 한국어 요약

### 핵심 (Core Concept)
스페인어 현재 시제 규칙 동사는 **-ar, -er, -ir** 세 가지 그룹으로 나뉘며, 각 그룹마다 고유한 어미 변화 패턴이 있습니다. 영어와 달리 스페인어는 **주어에 따라 동사 형태가 변합니다** (활용).

### 구조 (Structure/Pattern)
| 인칭 | -ar 동사 (hablar) | -er 동사 (comer) | -ir 동사 (vivir) |
|------|-------------------|------------------|------------------|
| yo | hablo | como | vivo |
| tú | hablas | comes | vives |
| él/ella/usted | habla | come | vive |
| nosotros/as | hablamos | comemos | vivimos |
| vosotros/as | habláis | coméis | vivís |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hablan | comen | viven |

### 주의사항 (Cautions)
1. **주어 생략 가능**: 동사 형태만으로 주어가 명확하므로 대명사(yo, tú 등)를 생략하는 경우가 많음
2. **vosotros vs ustedes**: 스페인에서는 vosotros, 중남미에서는 ustedes 사용
3. **-er/-ir 유사성**: nosotros/as 형태만 다르고 나머지는 거의 동일

- 현재 진행형 (estar + gerundio)

## Introduction

### What is the Present Tense?

The **present tense** (el presente de indicativo) is the most fundamental verb tense in Spanish. It describes:
- Actions happening **right now** (*I eat*)
- **Habitual actions** (*I eat breakfast every day*)
- **General truths** (*Water boils at 100°C*)
- **Near future plans** (*I leave tomorrow*)

### Why It Matters

In English, verbs barely change: *I eat, you eat, we eat, they eat* (only *he/she eats* adds an -s). 

In Spanish, **every person has a unique ending**. This means:
- You can often **drop the pronoun** (yo, tú, él) because the verb tells you who's doing the action
- You **must learn the patterns** to be understood
- Once you know the three patterns (-ar, -er, -ir), you can conjugate **thousands of verbs**

### Quick Comparison

| English | Spanish | Note |
|---------|---------|------|
| I speak | Yo hablo | -o ending for "I" |
| You speak | Tú hablas | -as/-es ending for "you" |
| He speaks | Él habla | -a/-e ending for "he/she" |
| We speak | Nosotros hablamos | -amos/-emos/-imos for "we" |
| They speak | Ellos hablan | -an/-en ending for "they" |

## Core Explanation

### The Three Verb Groups

All Spanish infinitives end in **-ar**, **-er**, or **-ir**. This ending determines the conjugation pattern.

| Group | Example | Meaning | Percentage |
|-------|---------|---------|------------|
| **-ar** | hablar | to speak | ~70% |
| **-er** | comer | to eat | ~20% |
| **-ir** | vivir | to live | ~10% |

> **Good news**: The patterns are **regular and predictable**. No surprises!

---

### Step-by-Step Conjugation Process

**Step 1: Remove the infinitive ending**
- hablar → **habl-**
- comer → **com-**
- vivir → **viv-**

**Step 2: Add the appropriate ending for each person**

#### -AR Verbs (hablar - to speak)

| Person | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|--------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | -o | **hablo** | AH-bloh |
| tú | -as | **hablas** | AH-blahs |
| él/ella/usted | -a | **habla** | AH-blah |
| nosotros/as | -amos | **hablamos** | ah-BLAH-mohs |
| vosotros/as | -áis | **habláis** | ah-BLAH-eess |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -an | **hablan** | AH-blahn |

#### -ER Verbs (comer - to eat)

| Person | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|--------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | -o | **como** | KOH-moh |
| tú | -es | **comes** | KOH-mehs |
| él/ella/usted | -e | **come** | KOH-meh |
| nosotros/as | -emos | **comemos** | koh-MEH-mohs |
| vosotros/as | -éis | **coméis** | koh-MEH-eess |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -en | **comen** | KOH-mehn |

#### -IR Verbs (vivir - to live)

| Person | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|--------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | -o | **vivo** | BEE-boh |
| tú | -es | **vives** | BEE-behs |
| él/ella/usted | -e | **vive** | BEE-beh |
| nosotros/as | -imos | **vivimos** | bee-BEE-mohs |
| vosotros/as | -ís | **vivís** | bee-BEES |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -en | **viven** | BEE-behn |

---

### Key Patterns to Notice

1. **Yo form always ends in -o** (all three groups)
2. **Tú form adds -s** to the él/ella form (-as, -es, -es)
3. **Nosotros form is the only one with accent mark** on the ending (-amos, -emos, -imos)
4. **-ER and -IR are nearly identical** — only difference is **nosotros** (-emos vs -imos) and **vosotros** (-éis vs -ís)
5. **Él/ella/usted and ellos/ellas/ustedes share endings** within each group

---

### When to Use Present Tense

| Use Case | Example | Translation |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Current action** | Leo un libro | I'm reading a book / I read a book |
| **Habit/Routine** | Estudio español cada día | I study Spanish every day |
| **Fact/Truth** | El sol sale por el este | The sun rises in the east |
| **Near future** | Mañana viajo a Madrid | Tomorrow I'm traveling to Madrid |
| **Restaurant/Shop** | ¿Me da la cuenta? | Will you give me the check? |

> **Note**: Spanish doesn't distinguish "I do" vs "I am doing" — both use present tense. For emphasis on *right now*, use *estar + gerundio* (estoy leyendo).
