## 🇰🇷 한국어 요약

### 핵심 (Core Concept)
스페인어에는 '이다'라는 뜻을 가진 동사가 **두 개** 있습니다: **ser**와 **estar**입니다. 영어의 'to be' 하나만 쓰이는 것과 달리, 스페인어에서는 **본질/정체성(ser)**인지 **상태/조건(estar)**인지에 따라 구별해서 써야 합니다.

### 구조 (Structure/Pattern)
| 구분 | 동사 | 용도 | 예시 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 본질/정체성 | **ser** | 영구적 특성, 직업, 국적, 시간 | Soy estudiante (나는 학생이다) |
| 상태/조건 | **estar** | 일시적 상태, 위치, 감정, 건강 | Estoy cansado (나는 피곤하다) |

### 주의사항 (Cautions)
1. **형용사의 의미가 바뀝니다**: *ser aburrido* (지루한 사람) vs *estar aburrido* (심심한 상태)
2. **위치는 항상 estar**: 사건이 열리는 장소라도 위치를 나타내면 estar 사용
3. **죽음은 estar**: 영구적 상태지만 *estar muerto* (죽어 있다)로 표현

## Introduction

### What is This?

In English, we have **one** verb "to be" that does everything:
- I **am** a teacher.
- I **am** tired.
- The book **is** on the table.
- It **is** 3:00.

In Spanish, there are **two** verbs for "to be":
- **Ser** — for **identity, essence, permanent traits**
- **Estar** — for **states, conditions, locations, temporary situations**

### Why It Matters

Using the wrong verb can completely change the meaning of your sentence — or make you sound like you're saying something weird. This is one of the **most important grammar distinctions** in Spanish. Master it early, and everything else gets easier.

### Quick Comparison to English

| English | Spanish (Ser) | Spanish (Estar) |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| "She is smart" (trait) | Ella **es** lista | — |
| "She is ready" (state) | — | Ella **está** lista |
| "The apple is green" (variety) | La manzana **es** verde | — |
| "The apple is green" (unripe) | — | La manzana **está** verde |

## Core Explanation

### The Fundamental Rule

> **Ser = WHO/WHAT something IS (essence)**  
> **Estar = HOW/WHERE something IS (state/location)**

Think of it this way:
- **Ser** describes **identity** — things that define what something *is*
- **Estar** describes **condition** — things that describe *how* something is *right now*

---

### When to Use SER (The "Permanent" Things)

#### 1. Identity & Definition
- **Name**: *Yo soy María.* (I am María.)
- **Nationality/Origin**: *Soy estadounidense.* (I am American.)
- **Profession/Occupation**: *Él es médico.* (He is a doctor.)
- **Religion/Political affiliation**: *Somos católicos.* (We are Catholic.)

#### 2. Inherent Characteristics (Physical & Personality)
- *Ella es alta.* (She is tall.)
- *El coche es rojo.* (The car is red.)
- *Mi hermano es simpático.* (My brother is nice/friendly.)

#### 3. Time, Date, and Events
- *Son las tres.* (It is 3:00.)
- *Hoy es lunes.* (Today is Monday.)
- *La fiesta es en mi casa.* (The party is at my house — **event location**)

#### 4. Possession & Relationships
- *El libro es de Juan.* (The book is Juan's.)
- *Ellos son mis padres.* (They are my parents.)

#### 5. Material/Composition
- *La mesa es de madera.* (The table is made of wood.)

---

### When to Use ESTAR (The "Temporary" Things)

#### 1. Location (Physical Position)
- *Estoy en Madrid.* (I am in Madrid.)
- *Las llaves están en la mesa.* (The keys are on the table.)
- *¿Dónde está el baño?* (Where is the bathroom?)

> **Important**: Even if a building has been in the same place for 500 years, you use **estar** for its location. *La catedral está en el centro.* (The cathedral is in the center.)

#### 2. Physical & Emotional States
- *Estoy cansado.* (I am tired.)
- *Ella está enferma.* (She is sick.)
- *Estamos felices.* (We are happy.)
- *¿Cómo estás?* (How are you?)

#### 3. Temporary Conditions
- *La sopa está fría.* (The soup is cold — right now.)
- *El café está caliente.* (The coffee is hot — right now.)
- *La puerta está abierta.* (The door is open — current state.)

#### 4. Progressive Actions (Doing Something Right Now)
- *Estoy estudiando.* (I am studying.)
- *Ellos están comiendo.* (They are eating.)
- Formed with **estar + gerundio** (-ando/-iendo)

#### 5. Results of Actions
- *La cena está preparada.* (Dinner is prepared/ready.)
- *El trabajo está terminado.* (The work is finished.)

---

### The "Meaning-Changing" Adjectives

This is where ser vs estar gets **really interesting**. Some adjectives change meaning completely depending on which verb you use:

| Adjective | **Ser** (Trait) | **Estar** (State) |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **aburrido** | boring (personality) | bored (feeling right now) |
| **interesado** | self-interested / selfish | interested (in something) |
| **listo** | smart/clever | ready |
| **verde** | green (color variety) | unripe / green (not ready) |
| **maduro** | mature (personality) | ripe (fruit) |
| **malo** | bad/evil | sick / poorly made |
| **bueno** | good (character) | tasty / attractive |
| **rico** | rich (money) | delicious |
| **pálido** | pale (natural complexion) | pale (from shock/illness) |
| **orgulloso** | proud (arrogant) | proud (of something) |

**Examples:**
- *Juan **es** aburrido.* = Juan is a boring person.
- *Juan **está** aburrido.* = Juan is bored right now.
- *La manzana **es** verde.* = The apple is a green variety (Granny Smith).
- *La manzana **está** verde.* = The apple is unripe.
