# 🌍 Culture

Language and culture are inseparable. These posts explore the cultural context behind the words — what people eat, how they celebrate, what they value.

## Why this matters

Vocabulary you learn in a vacuum fades fast. Vocabulary tied to a specific place, dish, or tradition stays. A learner who knows *asado* AND has watched a Buenos Aires family grill on Sunday will remember the word forever.
