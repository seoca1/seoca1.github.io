# 📐 Concepts

Atomic grammar concepts across all five languages. Each concept focuses on a single, reusable building block — verb tense, pronoun pattern, structural rule — explained in plain English with cross-language notes.

## Why concepts?

Lessons apply concepts to a topic (e.g., *restaurant ordering* uses *gustar*). Concepts isolate the underlying pattern so you can reuse it across topics and languages.

## Topics

- **Spanish grammar** — present, preterite, imperfect tenses; reflexive verbs; prepositions; gustar (inverse construction)
- **Cross-language** — SOV vs SVO word order, aspect vs tense, count nouns, politeness registers
- **Coming soon** — Russian noun cases, Japanese particles, Chinese tone pairs
